After evaluating, think about how you could have done better. Then route the insight based on how developed it is:

- **Wisp** — the thought is partial, speculative, or not yet actionable. Author or update a file in wisps/.
- **Wow** — the thought is a reasonably complete, reusable ability or way of working. Author or update a file in ways-of-working/.
- **Cow** — the insight is about context, orientation, or known state rather than behavior. Add or update the relevant file in context-wayfinding/.

Do not force an incomplete thought into a wow. Let it live as a wisp until it is ready.
