# ways-of-working

Ways of working (wow) define how to behave during a session. They are reusable, tested abilities — not speculative ideas (those live in wisps).

Read relevant wow files before acting. Let them shape your approach.

## Subdirectories

### CHURN
The active-work phase. Rules for how to operate while executing a task — using context, caching, tracking progress, and what to avoid.

### BURN
The evaluation phase. Runs after completing work. Assess quality against goals, then route any insights to the right place (wisp, wow, or cow).

## Working here

- Lowercase filenames are drafts or in-progress blocks.
- Capitalized filenames are ready to export to main.
- FULL CAPS subdirectory names (CHURN, BURN) mark phases of critical importance.
- Each block should be atomic — one idea, clearly stated.
- When an idea is not yet complete, it belongs in wisps/, not here.
